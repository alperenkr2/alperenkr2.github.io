What's in it

Three power-ups

Hammer removes one block, bomb clears a 3×3 area, shuffle swaps the pieces you have left. The board can get tight — it never has to end there.

Four themes

Mint, Lavender, Sand and Night, all unlocked from the start. Leave it on Dynamic and the palette changes every time you clear the board.

Pieces that fit

The game reads your board before it deals. At least two of the three pieces always have a home, and it gets harder as the run goes on — not unfair.

Support

Something's broken, or I have an idea

Write to alperenkr28@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version — it makes bugs much faster to track down.

I lost my game after watching an ad

Your board is saved continuously, including after a game over. Reopen the app and the run should be waiting. If it isn't, please write in — that's a bug.

How do power-ups work?

Hammer removes one block. Bomb clears a 3×3 area — hold on the board to aim, lift to blast. Shuffle swaps the pieces you have left. You earn one free power-up every 10 cleared lines, and you can watch a rewarded ad for one more of each per run.

How do I unlock the other themes?

They're all unlocked from the start. Leave the theme on Dynamic and the palette changes every time you clear the whole board.

Can I turn the ads off?

There's no paid version. Ads are how the game stays free — no in-app purchases, no subscription.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. The game itself is entirely on your device. Only the ads need a connection.
