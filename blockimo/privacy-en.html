Privacy Policy

Blockimo · Last updated 9 September 2026

Blockimo is a block puzzle game for iPhone. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

What the app itself collects

Nothing. Blockimo has no account system, no login and no server of its own. Your score, your saved board, your power-ups and your settings are stored only on your device, and they are deleted when you delete the app. Nothing you do in the game is sent to us, because there is nowhere for it to be sent.

Advertising

Blockimo is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To choose and measure those ads, AdMob may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) and other device identifiers
  • ad interaction and app usage data — which ads were shown, tapped, or skipped
  • approximate location derived from your IP address
  • crash and performance diagnostics

This data is handled by Google, not by us. Google's practices are described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Tracking and your choice

The first time you open the app, iOS asks whether Blockimo may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you decline, the advertising identifier is not shared and you will see less relevant ads instead. The game itself works exactly the same either way — nothing is locked, slowed down or hidden based on your answer.

You can change your mind at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Children

Blockimo is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
